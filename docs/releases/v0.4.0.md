# v0.4.0 — Suburb, rides and the beach town

The street now changes by week, and it looks finished. It runs on the beta address; the live address still shows "coming soon".

## What's new

- **Neighborhoods by week.** Week 1 is Maple Street, the suburb. Week 2 is the beach town. Then the cycle repeats while the difficulty keeps climbing. The banner at the start of a shift names the week and the neighborhood.
- **The beach town.** Pastel houses under striped roofs, wooden decks, palm trees, sand, a boardwalk and the sea. Its own obstacles: sand drifts that slow the ride, surfers carrying a board across the street, and golf carts drifting from lane to lane. No dogs there.
- **A finished suburb.** Three house shapes, more wall colours, shutters, window boxes on houses that ordered, three kinds of tree, fences, street lamps and yard props. Some traffic is pickup trucks, and the shop at the end of the street is fuller.
- **Finished rides**, and a picture of each real 3D ride in the garage.
- **Evening shifts.** Friday, Saturday, Sunday and the bonus round are played at dusk: lights on in the houses that ordered, glowing street lamps, light on the road ahead. The Payton Pizza van's sign lights the street. The rules are the same as by day.
- **A lower camera.** 57 degrees down, where it was 62, so more of the house fronts shows. It still never turns.
- **A slightly slower street.** 150 on the first Monday, gaining 7 a day up to 250 (was 160, 9 a day, up to 280).
- **Low effects**, in the pause menu, for slower phones.

## What's better under the hood

- `src/hoods.js` names the neighborhoods and says which one a day is in; a neighborhood's obstacles are switched on in `src/tuning.js`, and its look is a theme in `src/render3d.js`.
- Houses are built when a street starts and thrown away when the next one does, so memory does not grow as the weeks go by.
- The aiming ring works out where a pacing customer will be when the pizza arrives.

## Tested

- Cam played each part on his iPhone on 2026-11-23 and 24. He likes the beach town, called the lower camera "how it's supposed to be", said the evening shifts and the details "look so good", saw "no stuttering or slowness at all", and after the street was slowed said the speed and the beach obstacles both feel right.
- 93 unit tests, including every beach obstacle and which neighborhood a day gets, and every test street replayed start to finish.
- 8 browser tests at desktop and phone sizes.

## Known limits

- No frame-rate numbers were taken this time; Cam reported it by eye. The heaviest scene measured in a test browser drew about 44,000 triangles in 83 draws, a little more than the 40,000 in 49 his phone ran at 60 frames a second in v0.2.0.
- Only two of the four neighborhoods exist, so week 3 goes back to the suburb. Downtown and the snowy town come in v0.5.0.
- The sea only shows on a wide screen, not on a phone held upright.
- Bins, hydrants and cones keep their earlier shapes, and traffic has no headlights in the evening.
- Not tried on Android or on older iPhones.
