# v0.2.0 — 3D core and controls

Payton Pizza now plays on a 3D street. One fixed camera looks steeply down the street, tilted enough to see front doors, and never turns. Throwing is easier to control. It runs on the beta address; the live address still shows "coming soon".

## What's new

- The street, houses, customers, traffic, hazards and all four rides are drawn in 3D, every shape built in code.
- The whole street stays in view, lawn to lawn, so you can watch each pizza land.
- Aiming rings: a ring on each side shows where a pizza thrown now would land, green over a front door and gold over a customer's hands.
- A light tap always throws. A thumb that slips a few pixels no longer steers the ride instead. Chips in the bottom corners show where to tap.
- Front doors and hand-offs are bigger targets.
- Every player gets the same street and the same traffic on a given day.
- The black car is the Gun-Metal Black Bimmer, gun-metal black with no badge or logo.
- The game fills the whole window.

## What's better under the hood

- The rules run in fixed steps of 1/60 s and draw every random number from a seed, so the same inputs always give the same result.
- The five test streets from the build plan are recorded and replayed by the unit tests.
- A frame-rate readout (`?fps=1`) and testing switches for the street, day and ride.

## Tested

- Cam played it on his iPhone on 2026-11-23 and approved the camera angle, the width of the view, the taps and the aiming rings. The readout showed an average of 60 frames a second with one-second lows of 46 and 49.
- 49 unit tests, including 188 throws on a lit aiming ring that were all delivered, and every test street replayed start to finish.
- 6 browser tests at desktop and phone sizes, including a tap that slips.

## Known limits

- The busiest test street was not run on the phone on its own; the frame rate above is from ordinary play.
- Not tried on Android or on older iPhones. Shadows can be turned off with `?shadows=0`; a proper setting comes later.
- The hoverboard and dirt bike riders are small from above. The finished look for rides and people comes in v0.4.0 and v0.5.0.
- Lives, Continue, extra-life pickups and ride perks arrive in v0.3.0.
- The garage's ride pictures are still the 2D ones.
