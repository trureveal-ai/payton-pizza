# v0.3.0 — Deeper gameplay

There is now more to a run than surviving it. Lives carry through the week, a run that ends can be continued, each ride shrugs off more than the one before, and Sunday leads into a bonus round. It runs on the beta address; the live address still shows "coming soon".

## What's new

- **Continue.** When a run ends you can pick up from the start of the day you reached, with three lives. Your bank and rides are kept; your score starts again from zero.
- **Extra lives.** One or two hearts on every street, up to five lives. The banner at the start of a shift and the shift ticket show your lives.
- **Ride perks.** The dirt bike rides over potholes. The Gun-Metal Black Bimmer also knocks cones, trash cans and balls aside. The Payton Pizza van also shrugs off dogs and skaters. The garage and the shift banner spell this out.
- **Dogs chase from the side.** A dog barks, jumps out from the curb, runs alongside and closes in. Throw it a pizza: an orange ring shows when it is close enough to hit, and a pizza that lands on the ground nearby draws it away. Either way it stops to eat.
- **New customers.** One walks out to the curb. One is impatient and goes inside when their timer runs out; the front door still counts.
- **New hazards.** Puddles, balls that roll across the street, and roadwork barriers.
- **A bonus round after every Sunday.** Every house ordered, nothing in the way, free pizza, double tips and points.
- **An easier week.** Slower streets with fewer hazards early on, bigger pizza bags, pickups that fill the bag, and an aiming ring that goes out once a pizza is on its way so a second one is not wasted.

## What's better under the hood

- Every number that changes with the day is in `src/tuning.js`.
- `npm run measure` has an automatic player ride twelve streets a day and prints how it did, so tuning changes can be compared.
- `?bonus=1` in the page address starts in the bonus round.

## Tested

- Cam played it on his iPhone on 2026-11-23 through three rounds of changes. He called Wednesday to Sunday "just right", said the dog is "much better now", liked the bonus round, found Continue worked as he expected, and said the perks felt worth their price. It ran smoothly.
- 85 unit tests, covering every new rule, and every test street replayed start to finish.
- 8 browser tests at desktop and phone sizes.
- The automatic player went from finishing no street after Tuesday to finishing 11 or 12 of 12 from Wednesday to Sunday. It steers worse than a person, so this shows direction only.

## Known limits

- Cam has not finished Sunday starting from the Sunday test link, which gives three lives where a normal run could bring five. He chose to leave Sunday as it is for now.
- He reached the bonus round through the test link, not by finishing a week.
- Not tried on Android or on older iPhones.
- Dogs, balls and puddles are small from the fixed camera, and the garage's ride pictures are still the 2D ones. The finished look comes in v0.4.0 and v0.5.0.
